Here at Compt, we price our lifestyle benefits platform on a fixed annual tier based on your employee headcount, not a per-employee-per-month (PEPM) fee. Your software cost stays flat for the length of your contract, whether you add five people or 500. Compt also never requires you to prefund your employee benefit balances.
